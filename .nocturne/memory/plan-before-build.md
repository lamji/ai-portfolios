- 2026-11-13: after several rejected redesigns the user said "dont vibe code, research for best animated, ui, plan etc".
- **Why:** repeated build-first iterations (v2–v5) missed what they wanted and wasted their time.
- **How to apply:** for any new concept or redesign, research references first, present a concrete plan (structure, UI, motion, tech, milestones) and wait for approval; then build in milestones with check-ins. Related: [[v2-design-rules]].
